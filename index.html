About CDE

An established Qatari supplier, now with a systems division

Chemical Dynamics Enterprises WLL has a strong client base in Qatar. We provide supplies and services to the oil & gas industry, the power industry, the textile industry, the food industry, the pulp & paper industry and the process industry — for their process, water and wastewater treatment plants and laboratories.

We supply laboratory equipment and reagents, online analyzers, catalysts and support material, water and process treatment chemicals (open and closed cooling, boiler, wastewater and RO), absorbents and adsorbents, chemical dosing pumps and controllers, and measuring instruments for water and process industries.

We also supply and install automatic chemical dosing skids and controllers, along with other equipment and analyzers — and, through CDE Digital, the control and information systems that sit above them.
