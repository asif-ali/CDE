About CDE

A reliable supplier for your business

Our business has a strong client base in Qatar. We provide supplies and services to the oil & gas industry, the power industry, the textile industry, the food industry, the pulp & paper industry and the process industry — for their process, water and wastewater treatment plants and laboratories.

We supply laboratory equipment and reagents, online analyzers, catalysts and support material, water and process treatment chemicals (open and closed cooling, boiler, wastewater and RO), absorbents and adsorbents, chemical dosing pumps and controllers, and measuring instruments for water and process industries.

We also supply and install automatic chemical dosing skids and controllers, along with other equipment and analyzers.

Alongside our supply business, CDE now offers IT services to companies across Qatar — websites and applications, business email and support, e-invoicing readiness and branding — delivered with the same reliability our supply clients expect.
